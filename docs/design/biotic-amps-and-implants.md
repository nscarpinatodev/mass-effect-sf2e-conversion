# Biotic amps and implants: research notes

How the Mass Effect games handle biotic amps, implants and power recharge, and what that could mean for our cooldowns. Written 2026-11-16 as groundwork.

## Decided (2026-11-16)

- **Amps replace Charisma** in biotic cooldowns, for every biotic power. A power cools down for **4 rounds, less the amp's Recharge (−1 to −3), minimum 1**. Powers with a fixed cooldown of their own keep it: Biotic Charge (2 rounds, 1 with Improved Charge) and Lift Grenade (3).
- **Changed 2026-11-19:** a 1-round cooldown (Recharge −3) goes only to the first biotic power used each turn; any others that turn take 2 rounds. Nova is not counted and keeps its 1 round. See the Adept in `docs/class-audit.md`.
- **Power** comes in three steps, all item bonuses:

| Power | Biotic damage | Class DC | Barrier HP |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | +1 | — | +5 |
| 2 | +2 | +1 | +10 |
| 3 | +3 | +2 | +15 |

- **The line:** 16 amps in `me-items`, generated by `scripts/generate-biotic-amps.mjs`. The models are Solaris, Gemini, Unity, Polaris (which trades Power for barrier), Prodigy, Savant and HMBA.
- **Automation:** Vanguard cooldowns read the amp's Recharge (`flags.<module>.bioticRecharge`). The barrier adds `barrierBonus` when raised. Biotic power damage takes the higher of `ampDamageBonus` and the Power Amplifier's `powerDamageBonus`.
- **Implants (L2/L3/L5)** are a later step.

The rest of this file is the research that led here.

## Implants vs. amps (lore)

- **The implant is the port; the amp is what plugs into it.** The codex describes the implant as a surgically embedded interface port that amps are "plugged in" to. In humans it usually sits at the base of the skull. Implants are also called "wetware".
- **Human biotics need both.** Eezo exposure gives a person biotic potential, but it takes training plus a surgically implanted amplifier to make mass effect fields strong enough to be useful.
- **Asari don't need implants.** They're naturally biotic and have voluntary control from birth. In ME1, though, Liara and Wrex still equip amps alongside Kaidan.
- **Turian biotics** are about as strong as a human L3, by the lore summaries.

### The L-series (human implants)

Dates differ between sources; these are the commonly cited ones.

| Model | Era | What it's known for |
|---|---|---|
| L1 | 2152 (shelved) | Failed trials; subjects were crippled or killed. |
| **L2** | 2160 → replaced ~2170–73 | The first working implant (Conatix, BAaT program). It can **spike to more power than an L3**, but causes migraines and, in bad cases, crippling pain or insanity. Kaidan has one. The biotic rights movement in ME1 seeks reparations for L2 victims. Swapping an L2 for an L3 is possible but risks brain damage. |
| **L3** | ~2170 onward | The Alliance standard: **safe but moderate**, with a lower ceiling than an L2. Most human biotics in the trilogy have one. |
| **L4 / L5** | by 2186, experimental | Newer Aldrin Labs and Menton Research models with more power, not yet widely used. One fan source ties the L4 to the Ascension Project. |

**Takeaway for design:** an implant is a *permanent part of the character*: high-variance L2, steady L3, rare and experimental L4/L5. An amp is *gear* you swap and upgrade.

## Mass Effect 1: bio-amps as gear

The Adept, Vanguard and Sentinel each have an amp slot, as do Liara, Kaidan and Wrex. Every amp has three stats, all percentages:

- **Power:** more Throw and Lift force, more Warp damage, and more **Barrier strength**.
- **Cooldown:** shorter recharge for every biotic talent.
- **Duration:** longer-lasting biotic effects.

Each model comes in levels I–X. Power and Duration stay fixed for a model; only **Cooldown climbs** with the level. Each manufacturer has a clear personality:

| Amp | Maker | Levels | Duration | Power | Cooldown (low → high level) | Personality |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| Solaris | Aldrin Labs | I–X | +5% | +10% | +5% → +20% | Entry-level, a bit of everything |
| Gemini | Armax Arsenal | I–X | — | +5% | +15% → +30% | **Recharge** |
| Unity | Sirta Foundation | mid levels* | +15% | +5–20%* | +15% → +23% | Balanced |
| Polaris | Kassa Fabrication | IV–X | +35% | +10% | +20% → +30% | **Duration** |
| Prodigy | Armali Council (asari) | V–X | +25% | — | +32% → +40% | **Recharge and duration** |
| Savant | Serrice Council (asari) | VII–X | +35% | **+40%** | +35% → +40% | Top tier, **all three**, power-heavy |
| HMBA Basic / Advanced / Master | Spectre gear | — | +15–17% | +20–22% | +23% → +33% | Spectre-exclusive, balanced |

\*The two Fextralife pages for Unity disagree on its Power bonus and level range.

**What stands out:**
- Recharge is the stat every amp has, and it scales with quality.
- Power is the rare, premium stat: only Savant has a lot of it.
- Asari-made amps (Armali, Serrice) are the high end.
- In ME1, Power also strengthens **Barrier**, which ties the amp directly to how big a Vanguard's barrier, and so its Nova, can be.

## Mass Effect 2: amp *upgrades*, not amp items

There's no amp slot. Research upgrades on the Normandy split the same three ideas into separate upgrades:

| Upgrade | Effect |
|---|---|
| Hyper-Amp (6 levels) | +10% biotic damage each |
| Neural Mask | +20% biotic duration |
| Smart Amplifier | +20% faster biotic cooldowns |

## Mass Effect 3: recharge comes from what you carry

- **Weight drives recharge.** Each power has a base recharge time. Shepard starts at **+200% recharge speed** with nothing carried, and every weapon's weight eats into that bonus. Passives raise capacity, and upgraded weapons weigh less. Fan guides measure the +200% baseline; BioWare never published the formula.
- **Stacking formula:** recharge bonuses add together and divide the base: time = base ÷ (1 + sum of bonuses). Each extra bonus therefore helps less than the one before.
- **Armor:** some armor pieces add recharge speed.
- **Power evolutions:** many ranks offer a choice between recharge and damage.
- **Lore only:** the "Biotic Amp Interfaces" side mission has you recover better amp interfaces for an asari, with no gameplay stats.

## What we have today

- **Cooldowns:** Vanguard powers use *5 − Charisma* rounds (minimum 1). Biotic Charge is a fixed 2 rounds, or 1 with Improved Charge.
- **Biotic gear in the compendium:**
  - *Power Amplifier I/II* armor mods: +1/+2 to tech and biotic power damage, with working rules.
  - *Biotic Feedback Generator* (L7): +2 class DC for biotics. Its text says it needs "an active biotic implant", but no implant item exists.
  - *Recharge Module I/II* armor mods (L5/L9): "Reduces power recharge time by 1/2 rounds", **text only**. No rules, and the Vanguard cooldown automation ignores them.
- **No Biotic Amp item and no implant item exist.**

## Mapping the games onto our rules (options, not decisions)

**Cooldown as rounds.** ME1's +20–30% cooldown on a 5-round cooldown is about one round; +35–40% is about two. So an amp's "Recharge" could simply be **−1 or −2 rounds** to biotic cooldowns, with a minimum of 1.

**Where Charisma fits.** Three ways to go:
1. **Keep 5 − Charisma, and let amps subtract on top.** This is the smallest change. Strength Vanguards still lag Charisma ones.
2. **Amps replace Charisma.** Use a flat base, for example 4 rounds, minus the amp's Recharge. Cooldowns become a gear decision, like ME1. A Strength Vanguard with a recharge amp keeps up.
3. **Split by power.** Nova, Rush and the other "big" powers use a base minus the amp, while lesser powers keep Charisma.

**Power.** This could be an item bonus to biotic power damage, along the lines of the Power Amplifier mods. Following ME1, it could also raise **Biotic Barrier HP** (for example +5 per Power step), which feeds Nova's dice directly.

**Duration.** This could add +1 round to biotic conditions and effects such as Lift, Stasis and Pull. It's less valuable for a Vanguard.

**Implants.** These could be character-defining rather than gear. An amp slot needs an implant, or being asari or another natural biotic.
- **L2:** stronger spikes (for example, once per encounter a power deals extra damage or adds +1 to its DC), with a migraine drawback such as a flat check or stupefied 1 afterward.
- **L3:** the standard implant; no bonus and no drawback.
- **L4/L5:** rare and experimental: a small, steady edge, with GM-gated availability.

**A possible amp line for our compendium**, keeping ME1's makers and personalities:

| Amp | Focus | Recharge | Power | Other |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Solaris (Aldrin Labs) | Starter | −1 at higher grades | +1 | — |
| Gemini (Armax Arsenal) | Recharge | −1, then −2 | — | — |
| Polaris (Kassa Fabrication) | Endurance | −1 | — | +1 round durations, or extra barrier |
| Unity (Sirta Foundation) | Balanced | −1 | +1 | — |
| Prodigy (Armali Council) | Recharge and duration | −2 | — | +1 round durations |
| Savant (Serrice Council) | Top-tier power | −2 | +2 to +3 | Late-game only |
| HMBA (Spectre) | Balanced | −1 to −2 | +1 to +2 | Spectre requisitions |

Higher grades would raise Recharge first, as ME1 does, so a character's choice of amp is about *what kind* of biotic they are.
